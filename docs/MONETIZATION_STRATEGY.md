# COLIN — Platform Monetization Strategy & Economic Architecture

This document details the revenue models, listing duration tiers, verification fees, and student transaction protection mechanisms implemented across the COLIN Student Accommodation Platform.

---

## 1. Property Owner & Host Revenue Streams

### A. Listing Duration Subscriptions
* **6-Month and 12-Month Listing Plans**: Hosts pay a periodic platform fee to publish and keep their properties searchable in the Student Discovery Engine.
* **Granular Bed Management**: Listings support multiple room and sharing configurations (Single, Double, Triple sharing) with real-time bed occupancy tracking.

### B. Homepage Spotlight & Search Priority Promotion
* **Homepage Carousel Spotlight**: Hosts can sponsor their properties to appear directly in the curated **Featured Accommodations** section on the COLIN homepage.
* **Search Results Elevation**: Premium placements display a prominent `SPONSORED` badge and rank higher in student searches and campus proximity queries.

### C. COLIN Physical Verification & Certification Audit
* **COLIN Verified Badge**: Property owners can request an on-site physical inspection.
* **50-Point Quality Audit**: Trained COLIN field representatives inspect fire exits, CCTV coverage, hygiene standards, structural integrity, and verified Wi-Fi bandwidth.
* **Trust Multiplier**: Properties bearing the COLIN Certified Shield enjoy higher conversion rates, greater student trust, and prominent filter badge eligibility.

### D. Multi-Tier Host Membership Plans
* **Free Tier**: 1 active property listing, standard search rank, community support.
* **Verified Host (₹1,499 / month)**: 3 active listings, priority support, verified audit eligibility, and lower commission.
* **Premium Partner (₹3,999 / month)**: Unlimited listings, homepage spotlight placement, top-ranked discovery placement, dedicated account manager, and real-time room analytics.

### E. Deal Finalization Commission
* **Success-Fee Commission**: A (7–9)% placement commission on the first month's rent or a flat one-time onboarding fee per closed student lease, reflecting COLIN's end-to-end tenant verification and vetting.

## 2. Student Experience & Transaction Safety

### A. Centralized Visit Concierge & Booking Token
* **Anti-Poaching Protection**: Direct host contact information is protected prior to visit completion to ensure tenant safety and platform integrity.
* **Escrow-Backed Slot Booking**: Students place a visit security deposit to reserve an in-person or virtual walkthrough conducted alongside a COLIN Field Concierge.

### B. 12-Hour Decision Window & 55% Refund Guarantee
* **Deliberation Period**: Following the physical visit, students receive a 12-hour exclusive decision window during which the room/bed is locked against other applicants.
* **Guaranteed 55% Refund Policy**: If the student decides not to proceed with the accommodation after the visit, 55% of the visit booking deposit is promptly refunded to their account. If the student proceeds to finalize the booking, 100% of the token is applied toward their initial rent deposit.

### C. Accommodation Concierge Services
* **Tiffin & Meal Matching**: Curated local meal plan integrations tailored to dietary preferences.
* **Virtual Tours & Peer Roommate Match**: 360-degree room inspections and compatibility-based student pairing.
